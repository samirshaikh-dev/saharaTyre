What is a remolded tyre?

expand_more
A remolded tyre (also called a retreaded tyre) is a tyre that has undergone a professional retreading process where a new tread layer is applied to a structurally sound casing. At Sahara Tyre Vapi, our remolded tyres undergo rigorous safety inspection before sale. They are an affordable, eco-friendly option that reduces waste while maintaining safety standards — ideal for budget-conscious vehicle owners and fleet operators in Vapi GIDC.

What is nitrogen inflation and why is it important?

expand_more
Nitrogen inflation replaces regular compressed air with dry nitrogen gas in your tyres. At Sahara Tyre Vapi, we offer nitrogen filling because it maintains tyre pressure more consistently — nitrogen molecules are larger and escape slower than oxygen molecules. This leads to more even tyre wear, improved fuel efficiency, better handling, and reduced risk of sudden pressure loss. Nitrogen inflation is especially beneficial for long highway drives and commercial fleet vehicles.
