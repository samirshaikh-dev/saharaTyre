## Warranty and authenticity

- New tyres: genuine manufacturer stock with a 3-5 year manufacturing-defect warranty; some patterns carry additional unconditional or road-hazard cover.
- Used tyres: workshop satisfaction warranty after the 5-point inspection.
- Remoulds: casing and tread-bond warranty, typically 6 months.
- Every new tyre has a 4-digit DOT week/year stamp; staff verify the date before mounting.
- Digital warranty registration is completed at purchase where the manufacturer supports it.

## Selection guidance

- Wet highway braking on NH 48: Continental UltraContact UC6, Michelin Primacy 4 ST, Goodyear Assurance TripleMax 2.
- Pothole and rough-road durability: Yokohama Earth-1, Bridgestone Sturdo, MRF ZLX, CEAT Milaze X3.
- Industrial and warehouse: BKT Maglift solid forklift, BKT Loader Special for JCB 3DX.
- Two-wheeler performance: TVS Eurogrip Protorq Extreme, MRF Zapper, Michelin Pilot Street 2.
