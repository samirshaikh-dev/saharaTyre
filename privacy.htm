Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026 • Effective date: January 1, 2026

info 1. Introduction

Welcome to Sahara Tyre GIDC Vapi ("we," "our," or "us"). We operate the website https://sahara-tyre.vercel.app/ and provide in-person automotive tyre sales, puncture repairs, 3D laser wheel alignment, computerized balancing, and commercial fleet services at our physical workshop in Vapi GIDC, Gujarat, India.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and safeguard it, and your rights when you browse our website or reach out to us via telephone, WhatsApp, or email.

database 2. Information We Collect

Our website is an informational portal designed to assist vehicle owners and fleet managers in connecting with our workshop. We do not sell tyres online or process online financial transactions.

  • Direct Inquiry Data: When you contact us via WhatsApp, phone call (+91-9377158216), or email, you may provide your name, phone number, vehicle model, tyre size, or service requirement.
  • Automated Browsing Data: Standard internet log data, including your IP address, browser type, device specifications, operating system, pages visited, time spent, and referring URLs.
  • Location Data: General approximate location (e.g., city/state level) based on IP address to display relevant route assistance to our Vapi GIDC workshop.

task_alt 3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely for legitimate business operations:

  • To provide tyre price quotations, stock availability, and service estimations.
  • To coordinate emergency puncture assistance or schedule fleet maintenance visits.
  • To guide drivers with turn-by-turn directions to our workshop in Phase 1 GIDC Vapi.
  • To analyze website traffic, diagnose technical errors, and optimize Core Web Vitals and user experience.
  • To prevent fraud, spam inquiries, and ensure cybersecurity.

cookie 4. Cookies & Third-Party Services

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance site functionality and measure performance.

A. Google Analytics 4

Google Analytics uses cookies to collect aggregated, anonymous statistics regarding visitor behavior. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

B. Google AdSense

Google AdSense uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on prior visits to our website or other sites on the internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or AboutAds.info.

C. WhatsApp Direct Messaging

When you click WhatsApp buttons on our website, you are redirected to WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.). Messages sent via WhatsApp are subject to WhatsApp's standard end-to-end encryption and privacy policies.

lock 5. Data Sharing & Protection

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize customer contact lists or personal data. We only share information when required by Indian law, law enforcement, or to protect the vital rights and physical safety of our customers and staff.
