## Fitment rules

- Read the tyre size from the driver-side door jamb placard or owner's manual, not from an online model lookup alone.
- Match load index and speed rating to the original fitment or higher; never lower.
- Keep overall rolling diameter within +/-2% when upsizing.
- On dual commercial axles, match tyre diameter within 6 mm and circumference within 19 mm.
- Replace rubber valve stems at every new tyre fitment; TPMS sensors require tool-assisted handling.
- Re-check wheel nut torque after the first 100 km on commercial vehicles.
