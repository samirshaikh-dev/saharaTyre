## Direct answer

Sahara Tyre sells four tyre tiers: brand-new tyres, 5-point tested used tyres, certified commercial remoulds, and heavy commercial truck tyres. It also supplies passenger car and SUV tyres, alloy wheels, tubes, valves and nitrogen inflation. Brands stocked include MRF, CEAT, Apollo, Bridgestone, JK Tyre, Michelin, Goodyear, Yokohama, Continental, BKT and TVS Eurogrip. Stock is not sold online; availability and pricing are confirmed by phone, WhatsApp or at the workshop.
