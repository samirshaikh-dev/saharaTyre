## Story

Sahara Tyre was founded in 2005 as a small shop in Phase 1 GIDC Vapi with a focus on honest, reliable and affordable tyre service. Over 18+ years it expanded into a full workshop covering new and used tyre sales, 3D wheel alignment, balancing, puncture repair, brake and suspension inspection, and fleet maintenance. Growth is described by the business as driven mainly by repeat customers and word-of-mouth referrals.
