## Service notes

### Alignment vs balancing
- Alignment adjusts suspension angles so wheels are parallel and perpendicular to the road. It fixes steering pull, off-centre steering and uneven shoulder wear.
- Balancing corrects rotating mass with rim weights. It fixes speed-specific steering or cabin vibration.
- Recommended interval for both: every 5,000-7,000 km, or after pothole impacts and suspension work.

### Puncture boundaries
- Tread-crown punctures up to 6 mm can be repaired with an internal mushroom patch.
- Sidewall cuts, gashes, impact bulges and punctures within 25 mm of the shoulder are refused for safety.
- Every repaired tyre is pressure-tested in a water tank before refitting.

### Tyre condition tiers
- New: 3-5 year manufacturer warranty; all axle positions.
- Used: 5-point safety tested; minimum 3 mm tread; best for city use and spare wheels.
- Remould: commercial drive/trailer axles only; 40-60% lower cost than new in published guidance.
- Full comparison: https://sahara-tyre.vercel.app/condition

### Commercial and fleet work
- Multi-axle trucks, tippers, trailers, buses, Hydra cranes, forklifts and JCB equipment are serviced.
- Dual tyres must match within 6 mm diameter and 19 mm circumference.
- Truck wheel nuts are torqued to 550-650 Nm in a criss-cross pattern.
- Fleet contracts cover GIDC Phases 1-4, Sarigam and Umbergaon.

## Not offered
- Online tyre purchase, payment gateway, shipping or home delivery.
- Repair of sidewall damage or impact bulges.
- Remoulds on front steer axles of buses or high-speed trucks.
- Appointment-only servicing.
