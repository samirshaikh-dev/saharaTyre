## Alignment and balancing

**What is the difference between 3D wheel alignment and dynamic wheel balancing?**
Alignment calibrates suspension geometry (camber, caster, toe) so wheels are perpendicular to the road and parallel to each other, preventing pull and shoulder wear. Balancing adds calibrated counterweights around the rim to remove high-speed vibration.

**How often should I get wheel alignment and balancing done in Vapi?**
Every 5,000-7,000 km or every 6 months. Check sooner after heavy pothole impacts, industrial road use or steering pull.

**Do I receive a computerized before-and-after alignment report?**
Yes. The 3D laser system produces a digital before-and-after report showing camber, caster and toe readings and the corrections made.

**What are the common warning signs that my vehicle needs wheel alignment?**
Pulling to one side on a flat straight road, an off-centre steering wheel, rapid or uneven inner/outer edge wear, and squealing tyres during gentle turns.

## Punctures and roadside help

**How does Sahara Tyre repair tubeless tyre punctures?**
Quick fixes use high-grade vulcanized rubber seal strips. Permanent repairs use an internal vulcanized mushroom patch after dismounting, buffing and cleaning, which seals the tread hole and protects the steel belts from moisture.

**Can a tyre with a sidewall cut or structural bulge be repaired safely?**
No. Sidewall cuts, deep gashes and impact bulges cannot be patched safely. The sidewall flexes continuously and a repaired sidewall risks a high-speed blowout. Replacement is advised.

## Used and remoulded tyres

**Are second-hand used tyres from Sahara Tyre safe to drive on?**
Yes. Every used tyre passes a 5-point check: minimum 3 mm tread, sound sidewalls, airtight bead, no steel-belt separation, and a pressure test up to 45 PSI. Used tyres include a satisfaction warranty.

**What is the difference between used tyres and remoulded (retreaded) tyres?**
A used tyre is a previously driven original tyre with remaining factory tread. A remould is an inspected casing fitted with a new factory-vulcanized tread layer using cold precure technology.

**How much money can I save with remoulded tyres and what mileage do they deliver?**
Remoulded commercial tyres cost 40-60% less than new tyres and deliver 70-80% of new radial mileage, lowering cost per kilometre for cargo fleets.

**Can remoulded tyres be fitted on passenger cars or family SUVs?**
They are recommended for commercial trucks, trailers, tractors, loaders and rural LCVs. For passenger cars and family SUVs at highway speeds, use new tyres or 5-point tested used original radials.

## Nitrogen

**What are the benefits of nitrogen tyre inflation for highway driving?**
Nitrogen molecules are larger than oxygen, so pressure leaks more slowly. Dry nitrogen runs cooler on hot highway trips, reduces summer pressure fluctuation, limits internal rim oxidation and helps casing life.
