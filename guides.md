## Common questions

**Which tyre condition is best for a personal car in Vapi?**
For expressway driving and family use, brand-new MRF, CEAT or Apollo tyres give maximum wet braking and full warranty. For city-only use on a budget, 5-point tested used tyres with 4 mm+ tread are a lower-cost option.

**Are remoulds safe and legal for commercial trucks in India?**
Yes. Cold precure retreading is legal and widely used by transport fleets. Fit remoulds on drive and trailer axles, and use new tyres on front steer axles.

**What minimum tread depth should a second-hand tyre have?**
Do not buy below 2.5 mm. Sahara Tyre tests used tyres to a minimum of 3.0-3.5 mm and rejects bead damage, inner-liner cracks and sidewall bulges.

**Can new and used tyres be mixed on one vehicle?**
Only if both tyres on the same axle match in tread depth and construction (radial vs bias). Matching pairs across an axle is the safe rule.
