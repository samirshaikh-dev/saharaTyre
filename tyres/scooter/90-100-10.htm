Fitment Advice & Size Verification

90/100-10 is the standard rear fitment for the Activa 3G through 6G, Jupiter 110/125, Access 125 and Pleasure 100 platform. Scooters use different front and rear sizes, so confirm the code moulded on your existing sidewall or the specification in the owner's manual before ordering - the front wheel on most of these scooters is 90/90-12. If you are unsure, ride the scooter to our Char Rasta workshop and we will verify the fitment on the spot.

Note: Always verify your vehicle door jamb sticker, fuel lid placard, or existing tyre sidewall markings before purchasing. For safe handling and speedometer accuracy, overall rolling diameter should remain within ±2% of original manufacturer fitment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Expert answers for 90/100-10 pricing, pressure, and warranty

They are the front and rear sizes of the same scooter, not alternatives. 90/90-12 is the narrow 12-inch front wheel tyre and 90/100-10 is the wider, stronger 10-inch rear wheel tyre. Fitting the wrong one changes the rolling diameter and spoils handling, so always match the code moulded on your existing sidewall.
New MRF, CEAT and TVS Eurogrip 90/100-10 radials start from ₹1,050 at Sahara Tyre GIDC, and 5-point safety tested used tyres are available at roughly 25% to 40% of the new price. Fitting, tubeless valve replacement and initial nitrogen inflation are all included in the quoted price.
Run 29 PSI at the rear when the scooter carries only the rider and 36 PSI with a pillion passenger, always measured cold. Highway friction on NH 48 and Daman Road raises pressure by 4 to 6 PSI, so never bleed a hot tyre - check first thing in the morning instead.
