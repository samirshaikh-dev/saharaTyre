Fitment Advice & Size Verification

100/80-12 is commonly fitted on 12-inch electric scooter and e-rickshaw platforms including the Ola S1 Pro, Ather 450X and TVS iQube. EV manufacturers change wheel and tyre combinations between variants and model years, so verify the size moulded on your current tyre or in the owner's manual rather than assuming. Because electric scooters carry 25 to 40 kg of battery weight and deliver peak torque from zero speed, the tyre must be the correct size and the cold pressure checked every month.

Note: Always verify your vehicle door jamb sticker, fuel lid placard, or existing tyre sidewall markings before purchasing. For safe handling and speedometer accuracy, overall rolling diameter should remain within ±2% of original manufacturer fitment.
