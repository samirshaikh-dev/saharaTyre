Fitment Advice & Size Verification

3.75-12 8PR is the heavy-ply 12-inch fitment used on electric rickshaws, passenger autos and cargo three-wheelers running 12-inch rims. Note that the widely used 90/90-12 on the same rim diameter is a much lighter scooter pattern - the 3.75-12 is the reinforced alternative for battery and payload duty. Confirm the code moulded on your existing tyre before ordering, and run 50 PSI at the rear when loaded, measured cold.

Note: Always verify your vehicle door jamb sticker, fuel lid placard, or existing tyre sidewall markings before purchasing. For safe handling and speedometer accuracy, overall rolling diameter should remain within ±2% of original manufacturer fitment.
