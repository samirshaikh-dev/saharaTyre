Fitment Advice & Size Verification

4.50-10 8PR is the heavier-duty fitment used on cargo three-wheelers such as the Piaggio Ape Xtra LDX, Ape City, Atul Auto Shakti and Mahindra Alfa Load. A cargo vehicle running this size must keep 45 PSI at the rear once it is loaded, and 35 PSI when empty, because the same casing is expected to work on wet, broken, oil-contaminated factory approach roads. Measure the pressure cold and re-torque the wheel nuts after the first 100 km with a new tyre fitted.

Note: Always verify your vehicle door jamb sticker, fuel lid placard, or existing tyre sidewall markings before purchasing. For safe handling and speedometer accuracy, overall rolling diameter should remain within ±2% of original manufacturer fitment.
