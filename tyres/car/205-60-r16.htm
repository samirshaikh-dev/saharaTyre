Fitment Advice & Size Verification

205/60 R16 fits the Renault Duster and Captur, the Honda Amaze and select Corolla Altis trims, and a number of Hyundai and Kia models that use the 205 mm section on 16-inch rims. It is a 10 mm step up in width from 195/60 R16 and the overall diameter moves only about 0.8%, so it stays inside the two percent tolerance - but a wider tyre always needs a 3D alignment afterwards to correct the scrub radius.

Note: Always verify your vehicle door jamb sticker, fuel lid placard, or existing tyre sidewall markings before purchasing. For safe handling and speedometer accuracy, overall rolling diameter should remain within ±2% of original manufacturer fitment.
