Fitment Advice & Size Verification

235/60 R18 is the full-size SUV size used on the Tata Safari, the Honda CR-V in higher trims and select Hyundai Tucson and Kia Telluride variants. A 60% sidewall on an 18-inch rim is the ideal balance for this class of vehicle - enough rubber to protect the alloy on broken roads, enough width for confident highway braking, and a 103V load index that carries 875 kg per tyre without over-flexing. The 235 mm section generally wants a rim at least 7.5 inches wide, and we verify the offset before fitting.

Note: Always verify your vehicle door jamb sticker, fuel lid placard, or existing tyre sidewall markings before purchasing. For safe handling and speedometer accuracy, overall rolling diameter should remain within ±2% of original manufacturer fitment.
