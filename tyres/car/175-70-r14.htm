Fitment Advice & Size Verification

175/70 R14 is the wider 14-inch radial used on the Fiat Punto, Nissan Sunny and older Hyundai Accent variants. Compared with a 165/70 R14 it adds 10 mm of section width, which improves cornering and braking but tightens the arch clearance, so check for any rubbing on full steering lock before committing to it. If your existing tyre is a 165/70 R14, this is a plus-size change of roughly 2.4% in rolling diameter, so we measure and confirm it before fitting.

Note: Always verify your vehicle door jamb sticker, fuel lid placard, or existing tyre sidewall markings before purchasing. For safe handling and speedometer accuracy, overall rolling diameter should remain within ±2% of original manufacturer fitment.
