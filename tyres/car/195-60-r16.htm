Fitment Advice & Size Verification

195/60 R16 is a 16-inch fitment used on the Toyota Corolla Altis in some variants, the Skoda Octavia, the Honda Amaze and selected Honda Ciaz trims. The 60% profile keeps the sidewall tall enough to take the broken-road hits on GIDC roads while sitting comfortably on a 16-inch alloy. If your placard shows 195/55 R16, the 60 profile is the more protected option and stays within a couple of percent on rolling diameter; we measure before fitting either way.

Note: Always verify your vehicle door jamb sticker, fuel lid placard, or existing tyre sidewall markings before purchasing. For safe handling and speedometer accuracy, overall rolling diameter should remain within ±2% of original manufacturer fitment.
