Fitment Advice & Size Verification

215/45 R17 is the low-profile 17-inch size used by the Volkswagen Virtus, Skoda Slavia, Maruti Fronx and the newer Honda City and Hyundai Verna variants. At a 45% profile the sidewall is short, so the alloy face needs protection from the sharp-edged potholes around GIDC - which is a strong argument for a pattern with a reinforced sidewall. Running pressures here are higher than on a 60-profile tyre, so always follow the placard. A 3D alignment after fitting is essential, because a low-profile tyre reacts far more sharply to toe error.

Note: Always verify your vehicle door jamb sticker, fuel lid placard, or existing tyre sidewall markings before purchasing. For safe handling and speedometer accuracy, overall rolling diameter should remain within ±2% of original manufacturer fitment.
