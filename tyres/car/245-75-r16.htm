Fitment Advice & Size Verification

245/75 R16 is the tall-profile 4x4 size used on the Mahindra Thar 3-Door, the Tata Sumo and the Force Traveller. At a 75% aspect ratio the sidewall is 184 mm tall, which gives the suspension real travel over the broken tracks and the sharp-edged potholes around GIDC Phase 1 and on the roads out towards Bhilad. The 111S load index carries 1,090 kg per tyre, which matters when the vehicle is fully loaded with passengers or cargo on a trail. The rolling resistance of a tyre this tall is higher, so a careful driver will notice a small fuel economy difference - that is the price of the off-road capability.

Note: Always verify your vehicle door jamb sticker, fuel lid placard, or existing tyre sidewall markings before purchasing. For safe handling and speedometer accuracy, overall rolling diameter should remain within ±2% of original manufacturer fitment.
