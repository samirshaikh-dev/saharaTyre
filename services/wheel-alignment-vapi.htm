Wheel Alignment Angle Diagnostic Reference

Alignment Angle Definition & Role Misalignment Symptoms Correction Procedure
Toe (In / Out) Inward or outward tilt of wheels when viewed from above Rapid shoulder feathering, steering wheel off-center Tie-rod adjustment to factory toe specification
Camber (+ / -) Inward or outward tilt of wheel when viewed from front Heavy inner or outer edge wear, vehicle pull to one side Strut/control arm eccentric bolt adjustment
Caster (+ / -) Forward or backward tilt of steering axis when viewed from side Wandering at highway speeds, heavy steering wheel return Subframe/strut tower alignment check
