Our Story

Built on Trust,
Grown by Experience

Sahara Tyre was founded in 2005 with a simple mission — to provide honest, reliable, and affordable tyre services to the vehicle owners of Vapi GIDC. What started as a small shop in Phase 1 has grown into one of Vapi's most trusted automotive workshops.

Over the past 18+ years, we have built deep relationships with local families, truck fleet operators, and GIDC-based logistics companies. Our growth has been driven entirely by word-of-mouth referrals and repeat customers — a testament to the quality of our work and the trust we have earned.

Today, Sahara Tyre offers a complete range of services — from new and used tyre sales to wheel alignment, brake inspection, suspension checks, and fleet maintenance. We continue to invest in the latest equipment and training to serve our customers better, every single day.

Do you have experienced technicians? expand_more

Yes. Our team includes mechanics and tyre specialists with over 10–15 years of hands-on experience in tyre fitting, wheel alignment, brake inspection, suspension work, and heavy-duty truck tyre services for GIDC fleet vehicles.

What is your customer satisfaction rate? expand_more

We maintain a 4.8 out of 5 Google rating from over 120 verified reviews. Our customers consistently praise our honest pricing, quick service, and friendly staff. Every puncture repair, wheel alignment, and used/remolded tyre is backed by our full quality guarantee and satisfaction warranty — if you're not happy, we make it right.

Do you serve both car owners and fleet companies? expand_more

Yes. We serve individual car, SUV, and commercial vehicle owners, as well as GIDC-based logistics companies and fleet operators. We offer scheduled fleet maintenance, bulk tyre supply, and priority roadside assistance for commercial clients.

Do you have any certifications? expand_more

Our technicians are trained and experienced professionals. We are an authorised dealer for leading tyre brands including MRF, CEAT, Apollo, and Bridgestone, and we follow industry best practices for tyre fitting, alignment, and vehicle safety inspections.
