Char Rasta vs J-Type Road: Which Sahara Tyre Branch Should You Visit?

By Sahara Tyre Workshop Team • Updated October 2026 • 5 min read

Sahara Tyre runs two full workshops in Phase 1 GIDC, and the most common question at the counter is a simple one: which one should I go to? The honest answer depends on what you drive and where you are coming from. This comparison sets out the access routes, hours, vehicle mix and peak times for both, so you can choose once instead of driving across the estate twice.

boltDirect Answer

Choose Char Rasta for cars, scooters and bikes, especially if you are arriving from NH 48, Gunjan, Chala or Daman Road, or need a later evening slot. Choose J-Type Road for trucks, tippers, trailers and factory fleets inside Phase 1 GIDC, near J-Type Bridge and Walkaroo International. Both branches do alignment, balancing, puncture repair, new and used tyres and retreads; the difference is access and vehicle mix.

Char Rasta: The Main Junction Hub

Char Rasta is the original workshop and the one most drivers picture. It sits at Shop No 07, Amidhara Complex, on the main Char Rasta junction near the CNG pump, with the Phase 1 GIDC PIN code 396195. That position makes it the natural stop for anyone entering or leaving Vapi on NH 48, Daman Road or Silvassa Road.

The practical advantage is time. A car alignment, a balancing job or a tubeless puncture is usually a short stop, and the branch has the widest spread of passenger-vehicle stock. It also opens at 7:30 AM and closes at 9:00 PM from Monday to Saturday, which makes it the only option for a late-evening tyre problem. Sunday hours are 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM.

The trade-off is that the junction is busy. If you arrive in the middle of a dispatch rush, you may wait behind commercial traffic even though you are in a car. For a guaranteed quick passenger-vehicle slot, come early morning or after 3:00 PM. The Char Rasta branch page has the address and contact details, and the J-Type Road visitor guide covers the other side of the estate.

J-Type Road: The Industrial-Side Workshop

The J-Type Road branch is at Plot no 2910, near J-Type Bridge, beside WALKAROO INTERNATIONAL, with PIN code 396191. It was opened because trucks and factory vehicles should not have to cross the estate to reach a tyre bay. The daily rhythm here follows the plants: early dispatch, mid-morning fleet arrivals, and a quieter afternoon.

Hours are Monday to Saturday 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM and Sunday 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with the direct line on +91-7490052234. The branch handles heavy commercial fitting, dual-tyre height matching, cold precure retreads for drive and trailer axles, and GST-compliant fleet invoicing alongside the usual alignment, balancing and puncture work.

For a private car, the branch is still perfectly usable, but it is a detour unless you are already in the estate. If you are unsure about the internal roads, send a live location on WhatsApp and the team will guide you in from J-Type Bridge rather than let you circle the blocks.

Access Routes Compared

From NH 48: leave the highway at Char Rasta. The main hub is immediately there. For J-Type Road, continue on the internal GIDC roads toward J-Type Bridge instead of trying to cut across from the highway.

From Vapi Railway Station: come through town to Char Rasta. That is the simplest route to the main hub, and it is also the route to J-Type Road if you continue into Phase 1 GIDC afterward.

From Daman or Silvassa: both approaches funnel into the Vapi GIDC road network near Char Rasta. Stop at the main hub if you are in a passenger vehicle; continue to J-Type Bridge if you are heading for the industrial units.

From within GIDC Phases 2, 3 and 4: J-Type Road is normally the shorter run, provided the internal roads are open. If a diversion is in place, call the branch rather than guessing.

Peak Times and Waiting

At Char Rasta, the busiest windows are mid-morning and late afternoon, when highway traffic and local commercial vehicles overlap. A passenger car puncture or balancing job is usually quick, but a four-wheel alignment takes longer, so the earlier you arrive the better.

At J-Type Road, the first hour after opening belongs to vehicles heading out on dispatch. If you are bringing a truck, arrive early and call ahead. If you are bringing a car, the late morning and early afternoon are usually calmer.

Sunday is quieter at both branches but closes earlier, so it suits a private vehicle with a puncture or a pressure check rather than a fleet job. After the monsoon, alignment demand rises sharply because pothole impacts knock camber and toe out of specification. Book your visit before the rush, not after.

What Does Not Change Between Branches

The safety rules are identical. A sidewall cut, a bulge or a puncture outside the repairable crown area is a replacement, not a patch. Every removed tyre stays your property, and scrap value can be adjusted against the bill if you leave it behind. Alignment is shown on screen before and after, and balancing is confirmed on the machine rather than guessed.

Warranties are also handled the same way: new tyres carry the manufacturer warranty, and the team helps with digital registration at the counter. If you are comparing workshop standards rather than branches, our guide on how to choose a tyre shop in Vapi sets out the checks that matter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Sahara Tyre branch is better for a car or scooter? expand_more

For a car, scooter or bike, Char Rasta is usually easier. It sits on the main junction near the CNG pump, so you avoid driving deeper into the industrial estate, and it stays open until 9:00 PM Monday to Saturday. J-Type Road is the better choice if you are already inside Phase 1 GIDC.

Which branch should a truck or fleet use? expand_more

J-Type Road is the industrial-side branch and is usually the more practical choice for trucks, tippers, trailers and factory fleets. It sits near J-Type Bridge and Walkaroo International, close to the transport yards and warehouses. Call +91-7490052234 before bringing multiple vehicles so bay space and tyre sizes are ready.

Do both branches offer the same services? expand_more

Both branches handle 3D laser wheel alignment, dynamic balancing, tubeless puncture repair, new tyres, tested used tyres, cold precure retreads and fleet work. The difference is access and daily vehicle mix, not the service standard or the safety rules.

What are the opening hours of each branch? expand_more

Char Rasta is open Monday to Saturday 7:30 AM to 9:00 PM and Sunday 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM. J-Type Road is open Monday to Saturday 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM and Sunday 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Can I walk in without an appointment at either branch? expand_more

Yes. Both workshops run a walk-in model, and no appointment is required for alignment, balancing, puncture repair or tyre fitting. Calling ahead only helps for fleets, retreads and uncommon tyre sizes.

Which branch is closer to NH 48? expand_more

Char Rasta is the closer and simpler approach from NH 48, because it sits at the junction where the highway traffic enters Vapi GIDC. From there, J-Type Road is a short internal drive toward J-Type Bridge.
