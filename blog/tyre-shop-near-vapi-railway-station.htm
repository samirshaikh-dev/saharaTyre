Tyre Shop Near Vapi Railway Station: Directions and Timings

By Sahara Tyre Workshop Team • Updated October 2026 • 5 min read

Vapi Railway Station sits on the town side of the GIDC, and most travellers who need a tyre fixed arrive on foot, by auto, or with a vehicle parked nearby. The nearest full-service workshop is Sahara Tyre at Char Rasta, roughly a ten-minute drive from the station. This guide covers the route, the arrival landmark, what can be done quickly, and what to do if you are working against a train time.

boltDirect Answer

Sahara Tyre Char Rasta is at Shop No 07, Amidhara Complex, Char Rasta, near the CNG pump, Phase 1 GIDC, Vapi 396195, about ten minutes from Vapi Railway Station. It is open Monday to Saturday 7:30 AM to 9:00 PM and Sunday 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM. Call or WhatsApp +91-9377158216. Walk-ins are welcome, and a crown puncture repair is usually the fastest job.

The Route From the Station

Exit the station toward the town side and head for Char Rasta. Do not route yourself through the industrial estate first; the workshop sits on the junction itself, so the town approach is shorter and simpler. As you near the junction, look for the GIDC CNG pump. The workshop is in Amidhara Complex beside that approach, not deep inside a factory lane.

If you are using a ride-hailing app, set the destination to Sahara Tyres GIDC Vapi rather than typing a generic tyre shop. Several small counters use similar names along the station road, and a generic search can drop you at a puncture stall that cannot handle alignment, balancing or warranty work.

For a driver coming from the station side, the Char Rasta junction is also the entry point to Phase 1 GIDC. If you later need the industrial branch, continue from Char Rasta toward J-Type Bridge; the J-Type Road visitor guide covers that route in detail.

Arriving on Foot, by Auto or With a Vehicle

On foot: carry only what you can handle safely. A bare wheel is awkward in station traffic, and a tyre alone is not enough to test a repair properly. It is usually better to bring the vehicle or send a photo of the tyre and sidewall marking on WhatsApp before travelling.

By auto-rickshaw: ask for Char Rasta near the GIDC CNG pump. That landmark is more reliable than a shop name, because the junction is well known to local drivers. Keep the workshop number handy in case the driver needs directions for the final turn.

With a vehicle: arrive with the tyre size visible or noted down, for example 195/65 R15 91H. If the vehicle has wheel lock nuts, bring the key. Without it, the wheel cannot be removed without risking damage, and the job will stall at the bay.

What Can Be Done Quickly

Job Typical time Notes
Crown puncture repair 10–15 min Quick plug; a permanent internal patch takes longer
Wheel balancing 10–15 min for four wheels Cures highway steering shake
3D wheel alignment 20–30 min Includes a before-and-after printout
Tyre replacement 20–40 min Depends on stock, size and wheel type
Pressure and valve check 5–10 min Ask for a cold-pressure reading

Times are workshop estimates, not promises, and a truck or a corroded alloy wheel can take longer. If you are catching a train, say so at the counter before the vehicle goes onto the bay. The team will tell you honestly whether the job fits your window rather than start work you cannot wait for.

What to Bring From the Station Side

Bring the vehicle documents if the tyre is being replaced under a warranty claim, and the original invoice if you have it. For a company vehicle, give the 15-digit GSTIN so the invoice is correct the first time. If you are only passing through Vapi, ask for a written job slip with the tyre details, because a warranty discussion later is easier with the size and invoice in hand.

Payment is straightforward: UPI, cash, debit and credit cards are accepted, and corporate accounts can be settled by NEFT or RTGS with a GST invoice. If you are unsure whether a repair is safe, read our guide on when to replace car tyres before you travel; a sidewall cut or bulge is a replacement even if the tread looks healthy.

If You Are Only in Vapi Briefly

Station-side tyre problems usually fall into three groups: a slow leak that needs a proper immersion test rather than a guess, a puncture that needs a permanent internal patch, and a vibration that needs balancing. A quick visual check is not enough for the first two. Ask for the water-tank leak test, and ask to see the removed object and the repaired area before the tyre goes back on the vehicle.

If the workshop is busy, the junction is not a good place to wait in a car with the engine running. Step into the complex, keep your belongings with you, and confirm the expected finish time before you leave the counter. Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Sahara Tyre from Vapi Railway Station? expand_more

Sahara Tyre Char Rasta is roughly a 10-minute drive from Vapi Railway Station under normal traffic. Take the town route to Char Rasta and look for the workshop at Amidhara Complex, directly near the GIDC CNG pump.

Can I walk from Vapi Railway Station to the tyre shop? expand_more

It is a practical walk only if you are travelling light and the weather is reasonable. For a wheel or tyre, use an auto-rickshaw or cab instead, because carrying a wheel through the station-road traffic is unsafe. Share a live location on WhatsApp if the driver is unsure of the exact shop.

What is the fastest tyre service near Vapi Railway Station? expand_more

A tubeless crown puncture repair is the fastest, typically around 10 to 15 minutes. Wheel balancing for a set of four takes longer, and 3D wheel alignment takes about 20 to 30 minutes. If you are catching a train, tell the counter staff your deadline before work begins.

Is there parking at the Char Rasta workshop? expand_more

The workshop sits in a commercial complex on the Char Rasta junction, so space is shared with surrounding businesses. Two-wheelers are easy to park. For a car, follow the team's direction on arrival, and avoid blocking the CNG pump approach.

What are the workshop timings near Vapi Railway Station? expand_more

Sahara Tyre Char Rasta is open Monday to Saturday 7:30 AM to 9:00 PM and Sunday 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM. It is a walk-in workshop, so no appointment is needed for puncture repair, balancing, alignment or tyre fitting.

Can I leave my vehicle and collect it after a train journey? expand_more

Ask at the counter before leaving the vehicle. Short-duration work such as a puncture or balancing is normally completed while you wait. For longer jobs, confirm the collection time and keep the job slip with you, since the workshop closes at 9:00 PM on weekdays and 6:00 PM on Sunday.
