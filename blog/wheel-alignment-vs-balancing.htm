Wheel Alignment vs Wheel Balancing: What's the Difference?

By Sahara Tyre Workshop Specialists • Updated March 2026 • 5 min read

Many car owners frequently confuse wheel alignment and wheel balancing, assuming they are the same service. In reality, they address two completely different mechanical systems of your car.

Quick Comparison Chart

Feature Wheel Alignment Wheel Balancing
What is adjusted? Suspension angles (Camber, Caster, Toe) Weight distribution around the wheel rim
Primary symptom Vehicle pulls to one side; off-centre steering Steering wheel vibrates at 70–100 km/h
Tyre wear type One-sided edge wear or feathering Patchy, cupped, or scalloped tread spots
Recommended interval Every 10,000 km or after hitting pothole Every 8,000–10,000 km or upon new tyre fitment

Why You Should Always Do Both Together

Performing wheel alignment alongside computerized wheel balancing ensures that your tyres roll dead-straight without pulling and rotate symmetrically without vibration. Doing both together doubles tyre lifespan and optimizes fuel economy.
