## Direct answer

Sahara Tyre sells three tyre condition tiers. Brand-new tyres offer full tread and a 3-5 year manufacturer warranty. 5-point tested used tyres offer verified 3-6 mm tread at a lower cost, with a workshop satisfaction warranty. Commercial remoulds use inspected casings with a new precured tread, cost less than new, and are restricted to drive and trailer axles.

## 5-point used tyre inspection

Every used tyre must pass:

1. Minimum 3.0 mm usable tread, measured at multiple points.
2. No sidewall cuts, bulges, impact blisters or ozone cracking.
3. Intact bead wire bundle with no kinks, tears or rim-seat damage.
4. No internal carcass or steel-belt separation.
5. Pressure test up to 45 PSI in a water tank.

Tyres below 3 mm, with shoulder wipe or with structural damage are rejected.

## Remould rules

- Cold precure retreads use a factory-cured tread strip bonded to an inspected casing.
- Fit only on rear drive, trailer, lift or tag axles.
- Do not fit on front steer axles of buses or high-speed trucks.
- Do not fit on passenger cars or family SUVs; use new or tested used original radials instead.
- Pull casings for retreading at 2.5-3.0 mm tread, before belt damage.
