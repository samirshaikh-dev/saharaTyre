Wheel Alignment & Balancing

Differences, service frequency, diagnostic reports, and warning signs

While both services improve ride quality and tyre longevity, they address different mechanical aspects:
  • Wheel Alignment: Adjusts suspension angles (camber, caster, and toe) so that the wheels meet the road at the exact manufacturer angle. It fixes vehicle pulling to one side and stops uneven tyre tread wear.
  • Wheel Balancing: Balances weight distribution across the tyre and wheel rim using precision counterweights. It cures steering wheel shake and body vibration at highway speeds (60–100 km/h).
Whenever you install new tyres or rotate them, performing both alignment and balancing is essential.
We recommend wheel alignment and balancing every 5,000 to 7,000 kilometers or every 6 months. In industrial corridors like Vapi GIDC, Gunjan, and Silvassa, regular heavy truck traffic, railway crossings, and monsoon potholes can jar suspension components out of true. Checking alignment after hitting an aggressive pothole or curb prevents premature tyre replacement.
Yes, absolutely. Transparency is a core pillar of Sahara Tyre. Our digital 3D laser alignment computer prints an exact diagnostic sheet illustrating your car's initial suspension geometry and the adjusted factory-spec parameters. You can see the camber, caster, and toe angles in clear visual charts.
Look for these telltale signs of suspension misalignment:
  • Your car pulls or drifts to the left or right when letting go of the steering wheel on a straight road.
  • The steering wheel sits crooked or off-center while driving straight.
  • Tread is wearing rapidly or unevenly on the inner or outer shoulders of your front tyres.
  • You hear tyre squealing on smooth tarmac while taking slow, sharp turns.
  • Your fuel consumption has noticeably increased due to rolling resistance.

Puncture Repair & Roadside Assistance

Vulcanized tubeless repairs, sidewall damage limits, and 20km mobile coverage

We follow industry-standard puncture repair methods depending on the hole severity:
  • Standard Vulcanized Strip Repair: Fast, 15-minute on-wheel external plug repair for minor screw and nail punctures in the tread crown.
  • Internal Mushroom Combo Patch (Cold Vulcanization): The tyre is dismounted from the rim, buffed from the inside, and a chemical vulcanized patch-plug unit is sealed in place. This fills the puncture channel completely, shielding internal radial steel belts from water infiltration and rust.
No, and ethical workshops will never patch a sidewall. Unlike the steel-belted tread, the tyre sidewall undergoes continuous high-frequency flexing under vehicle load. An impact bulge indicates snapped internal ply cords, while a cut compromises the carcass structure. Repairing sidewalls creates extreme risk of a sudden highway blowout. At Sahara Tyre, we prioritize passenger safety and will recommend reliable replacement options.
Yes, mobile assistance is available within 20km of Vapi GIDC! If you suffer a puncture or flat tyre on NH 48, GIDC industrial roads, Gunjan, Chala, Chanod, Imran Nagar, Salvav, Bhilad, Daman, or Silvassa, our mobile mechanic can come directly to your location with tools, jacks, and inflators.

Used & Remoulded (Retread) Tyres

5-point safety testing, CPKM fleet savings, and vehicle recommendations

Yes, rigorously tested and certified. Unlike informal roadside sellers, every pre-owned tyre at Sahara Tyre must pass our mandatory 5-Point Safety Inspection before entering our inventory:
  • Tread Depth: Verified minimum 3.0mm to 5.5mm usable remaining tread.
  • Sidewall Integrity: Zero impact bulges, cuts, or weathering fissures.
  • Bead Seating: Factory-intact rubber bead ensuring air-tight rim seal.
  • Belt Alignment: Zero internal radial belt separation or distortion.
  • Pressure Testing: Inflatable chamber test up to 45 PSI to verify internal casing strength.
All used tyres come with our shop satisfaction warranty.
The core distinctions are:
  • Used Tyres: Original factory tyres previously run on other vehicles that still retain 50% to 75% of their initial tread depth without retreading.
  • Remoulded (Retreaded) Tyres: A structurally sound premium commercial tyre casing (such as MRF or Apollo truck radial) whose original tread was worn down, precision-buffed, and fused with a brand-new factory-grade precured tread strip in a high-temperature vulcanizing chamber.
Remoulded commercial tyres typically cost 40% to 60% less than brand-new tyres. Modern cold precure retreads deliver 70% to 80% of the mileage of a new radial tyre, lowering your fleet's Cost-Per-Kilometer (CPKM) significantly. For transport operators running 10.00-20 or 295/80 R22.5 tyres, this translates into tens of thousands of rupees in yearly savings per vehicle.
We recommend remoulded tyres strictly for commercial cargo haulage, container trailers, tractors, tippers, and low-speed rural utility vehicles. For passenger cars, high-speed highway sedans, and family SUVs, we strongly advise either brand-new tyres or certified 5-point tested used original tyres to ensure peak braking distance, wet grip, and high-speed safety.
