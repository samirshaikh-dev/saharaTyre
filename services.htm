Frequently Asked Questions — Auto Services in Vapi

What's the difference between wheel alignment and wheel balancing?

expand_more
Alignment corrects the angles (camber, caster, toe) at which your wheels meet the road — it fixes pulling to one side and uneven tyre wear. Balancing corrects weight distribution within the wheel-tyre assembly — it fixes steering vibration at speed. Most vehicles benefit from both after a new tyre fitting.

Do I get a report after my alignment service?

expand_more
Yes — every alignment comes with a before-and-after measurement printout so you can see exactly what was corrected. Full transparency, no guesswork.

How do I know if I need alignment or just balancing?

expand_more
If your car pulls to one side or the steering wheel sits crooked when driving straight, that's alignment. If you feel vibration in the steering wheel or seat at highway speed, that's balancing. Not sure? We'll check both for free.

What equipment do you use for wheel alignment and balancing?

expand_more
Computerised laser alignment for camber/caster/toe correction, and electronic sensor balancing to detect weight imbalance in the wheel-tyre assembly — the same standard used by authorised brand service centers.

What types of tyres do you repair — tubeless or tube-type?

expand_more
Sahara Tyre repairs both tubeless and tube-type tyres for cars, bikes, trucks, and commercial vehicles. Our workshop is equipped with industry-grade patching and vulcanising kits for lasting repairs on all tyre types.

How often should I get wheel alignment done?

expand_more
We recommend wheel alignment every 10,000 km or whenever you notice uneven tyre wear, steering pull, or after hitting a major pothole. Regular alignment extends tyre life and improves fuel efficiency. Visit Sahara Tyre in Vapi GIDC for a quick alignment check.

What is computerised tyre balancing?

expand_more
Computerised tyre balancing uses electronic sensors to detect weight imbalances in the wheel-tyre assembly. Small weights are added to eliminate vibrations at highway speeds. This prevents uneven tread wear and ensures a smooth, vibration-free drive. Sahara Tyre uses modern computerised balancing equipment.
